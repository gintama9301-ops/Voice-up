# VoiceUp category guide

Petitions are organised into five issue groups, each with specific categories.

| Category group | Specific petition categories |
|---|---|
| **Roads & Transport** | Roads & Potholes; Road Safety & Traffic; Public Transport; Streetlights & Public Lighting |
| **Water, Waste & Environment** | Water Supply; Drainage & Sewerage; Garbage & Waste; Environment & Pollution |
| **Education & Healthcare** | Schools & Education; Hospitals & Healthcare |
| **Public Spaces & Utilities** | Accessibility & Public Buildings; Housing & Urban Planning; Parks & Public Spaces; Electricity & Power; Public Safety |
| **Community & Governance** | Civic Services; Digital Services; Animal Welfare; Other Community Issues |

## Creating a petition

In **Start a petition → Define your community**, choose the **Category group** first, then choose the **Specific issue category**. Only categories in the selected group will appear. Both selections are required. The title-based assistant can suggest a category, but the author can change it.

## Exploring petitions

In **Explore petitions**, the **All categories** dropdown displays categories under the five group headings. All 835 existing catalog entries are assigned to the updated categories. Petition levels and records remain unchanged.

## Data transparency

The 835-entry catalogue contains a mixture of source-linked public petitions and illustrative example records. Reclassifying a record does not change its verification status, supporter totals, workflow status, or photo evidence.

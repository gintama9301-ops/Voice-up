# Source and rights policy for VoiceUp

The downloaded photo collection begins **empty** in this ZIP. The number **570** is a collection target, not a count of verified photographs already in the package.

The photo importer pulls images only from Wikimedia Commons records with a recognised open licence (such as Creative Commons Attribution, Attribution-ShareAlike or CC0). It records each original file page, author, licence and licence URL, and stores modified/resized WebP files as site assets. Open-licence images may require attribution and ShareAlike compliance. Review each image before public deployment.

Eight road-condition images were directly uploaded by the user. Their photographer, reuse permission and exact location have **not** been independently confirmed; the site treats them as user-provided illustrative references for the local prototype. Two Getty Images-watermarked screenshots are excluded from the distributable website, because their reuse rights were not established.

Two hospital/streetlight issue images are **AI-generated illustrations** used solely when a licensed real photograph is unavailable. They must never be represented as genuine evidence. Other empty categories use openly marked SVG topic illustrations.

**A representative cover is not evidence.** Do not label an image from Assam or another country as a photo from Mohali or any other petition district unless source evidence clearly supports that location. Keep direct petition evidence separate from illustrative cover images.

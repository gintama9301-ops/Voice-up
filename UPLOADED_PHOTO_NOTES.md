# Uploaded road photographs

This version integrates ten user-provided screenshots into a local project photo catalogue.

- **8** photos are available as bundled local `.webp` covers, under `assets/uploaded-road-photos/`.
- **2** Getty-watermarked screenshots are preserved unchanged under `rights-review/not-for-publication/` and are **not displayed** in public petition cards. Obtain a suitable license before publication.
- The first **8** matching pothole/road-repair petitions have different uploaded covers, even while online.
- Another **161** relevant petition records have these bundled photos as offline fallbacks; online Wikimedia Commons references may replace them if they load successfully. Because only eight displayable photos were supplied, a unique image for all 169 road-related entries is not possible.
- Uploaded photographs are **reference imagery only**. Neither the incident location, photographer, nor usage rights have been independently verified. Do not present them as incident evidence or officially verified reports.
- The complete photo catalogue and rights notes are in `data/uploaded_road_photos.csv`. The road petition-to-photo mappings are in `data/uploaded_road_photo_assignments.csv`.

For full deployment, obtain appropriate image licenses and documented location/photographer credits.
